Apple Mail: Settings → Signatures → create one → paste. Untick “Always match my default message font” or it will strip the styling.
Gmail: Settings → General → Signature → paste. Gmail keeps inline styles but drops <style> blocks, which is why everything here is inline.
Outlook: paste into the signature editor. The accent rule is a table cell with a background colour rather than a border, because Outlook renders borders inconsistently.
