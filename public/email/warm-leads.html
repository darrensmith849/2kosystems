What I changed from your draft, and why

  • Typos only, first: “from from” → “from”, and 2ko → 2KO throughout.
  • “; Your preferred” → an em dash. A semicolon followed by a capital reads as a slip, and this is the line that sets the tone.
  • “offers … for online systems now too” — “now” appeared twice in one sentence. Dropped the second.
  • “in one’s processes” → “in a business’s processes”. Your call — “one’s” is more formal and may be deliberate. Say the word and I’ll put it back.
  • “off excel” → “off Excel”, and linked to the Get Off Excel page. That is a real product at a published price, so the link does work the email otherwise has to do.
  • Added {{course}} — “your Green Belt” beats “training” by a distance. It proves you have their record in front of you rather than a list.
  • Added a subject line and an opt-out. Your draft had neither; see below.

Merge fields

{{first_name}} — first name only. No record? Send “Good day,” exactly as you wrote it; a wrong name is far worse than none.
{{course}} — “Green Belt”, “Yellow Belt”, “Black Belt”, “Lean Six Sigma course”. If your records do not have it, fall back to “training”.
{{month}} / {{year}} — “March” / “2022”. If the date is uncertain, cut to “a while back” rather than guess. Being wrong about their own course undoes the whole opening.
{{company}} — subject line only.

Before the batch goes out

The opt-out is not optional. POPIA s69 does let you market to people whose details you took in the course of a sale — a paid course counts — but only with a refusal route offered every time. One line, as above, is enough.

Send from your own mailbox in batches of about 40 a day. A mailbox that has never sent bulk suddenly sending four hundred is the quickest way into spam, and 2ko.co.za is the domain your invoices leave from. No tracking pixel here on purpose — replies are the metric, and a pixel is one more reason to be filtered.

Confirm the sending address. This signs off contact@2ko.co.za with 021 527 0065, taken from 2ko.co.za and sixsigmasouthafrica.co.za. If these should go out from your own address instead, tell me and I will swap it.
