Warm lead emails

Two versions, because the list is two different audiences and the law treats them differently. Everything below is exactly what lands — same HTML, same widths. Nothing has been sent.

Read this before sending

POPIA section 69 lets you market to people whose details you collected in the course of a sale — past clients — provided you offer an opt-out every time. Old enquiries who never bought are not covered by that exemption, which is why version B leads by asking rather than pitching. Both carry an unsubscribe line; do not remove it.

Send from your own mailbox, in small batches, not through the app. A relationship email pushed through a transactional system looks like a campaign, and campaigns get read as campaigns.

Merge fields

{{first_name}} — first name only. If you do not have one, cut the greeting to “Hi there” rather than guessing; a wrong name kills the email.
{{what_they_asked_about}} — B only, one short phrase in their words (“a booking system”, “your training courses”). If the record does not say, use version A instead of inventing it.

Before you send the batch

Send one to yourself first and read it on your phone — that is the only render that counts. Then go in batches of about 40 a day from your own mailbox: a mailbox that has never sent bulk suddenly sending 400 is the fastest way to land in spam and damage the domain you also send invoices from. There is no tracking pixel in either version, which is deliberate — replies are the metric.
