## Every setting answers a rule from the audit

| Setting | Value | Rule it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Bidding | Maximise Clicks, R25 CPC ceiling | **B1** — no smart bidding without a conversion signal |
| Match types | Exact and phrase only, no broad | **C3** — broad was 95.4% of SixSigma's spend |
| Negatives | 74, at campaign level, day one | **C1/C4** — SixSigma had four, after R66,000 |
| Networks | Search only, partners and display off | **D3** — network bleed |
| Location | South Africa, **Presence only** | **E1** — the default serves anyone *interested in* SA, anywhere |
| Landing pages | `https://`, one per ad group | **F2** — SixSigma's ads still land on `http://` |
| Status | Paused on import | **A1** — nothing runs before tracking exists |

The training negatives matter more than usual here: this campaign shares an
account with a training brand, and someone searching "job card system training"
is not buying a R95,000 build.

## Launch order — do not shortcut this

1. **Create three conversion actions** in Google Ads: `enquiry`, `scope`, `brief`.
2. **Set the env vars** in Cloudflare and redeploy:
   `NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_ID`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_LABEL_ENQUIRY`,
   `NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_LABEL_SCOPE`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GADS_LABEL_BRIEF`.
   The site code is already wired — `Analytics.tsx` loads gtag, `ContactForm`
   and `QuoteBuilder` call `track()`. Every call is a no-op until these are set.
3. **Verify** a test enquiry registers in Google Ads.
4. **Import** files 1–7 in numeric order.
5. **Enable** the campaign.

Launching before step 3 would repeat, on a new campaign, the exact mistake the
audit charges people to find.

## What to expect

This is thin, high-intent demand — a few hundred searches a month in SA across
all 58 keywords, not the 290,000 impressions SixSigma pulls. Expect roughly
20–60 clicks a month. **The R200/day budget is a ceiling it will not reach**,
and that is correct: the constraint here is available demand, not budget.

Judge it on enquiries, never on clicks or impressions. At R95,000 a system, one
enquiry a month makes this campaign the best-performing thing in the account.

Do not "fix" low volume by adding broad match. That is precisely how the
SixSigma campaign ended up spending R14,444 on searches like `iq academy`.
