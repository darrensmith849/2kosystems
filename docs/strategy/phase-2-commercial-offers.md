# 2KO Systems — Phase 2 Commercial Offer Architecture

Status: Commercial design draft  
Date: 12 September 2026  
Depends on: `phase-1-strategic-decisions.md`

## 1. Commercial objective

Create a clear route from an operational problem to a measurable intervention and, where justified, an ongoing improvement relationship.

The commercial model is:

> **Diagnose the constraint. Select the right intervention. Prove the value. Embed the control. Keep improving it.**

2KO will not sell a predetermined technology. Every offer must answer five questions:

1. Which process is in scope?
2. What measurable problem exists today?
3. Is the constraint process, capability, technology, or a combination?
4. What result will indicate that the intervention worked?
5. What will prevent the improvement from decaying?

## 3. Offer sheet — Free Fit Call

### Best for

A buyer who knows something is wrong but is unsure which 2KO offer or division should handle it.

### Promise

In thirty minutes, determine the most defensible next step: a review, audit, fixed-scope product, pilot, training conversation, or no further action.

### Inputs required

- one named operational process;
- one observable symptom;
- a sponsor or process owner;
- a rough indication of frequency, volume or impact.

### Outputs

- fit/no-fit decision;
- recommended paid starting point;
- required attendees and artifacts for that next step;
- transparent price or pricing route.

### Exclusions

- no process map;
- no diagnosis;
- no written recommendations;
- no technical architecture;
- no speculative proposal.

### CTA

> Tell us what keeps going wrong.

## 4. Offer sheet — Half-Day Process Review

### Best for

A visible operational problem whose underlying cause has not yet been established.

### Promise

Half a day with the people who perform and own the work, followed by a concise memo naming what is failing, why it matters and what should happen next.

### Deliverables

- one process walked end to end;
- current control method recorded;
- principal failure and likely mechanism;
- classification: process, capability, technology or combination;
- build/do-not-build recommendation;
- recommended next engagement;
- three-to-four-page decision memo.

### Boundaries

- one site;
- one process;
- maximum four participants;
- no detailed financial model;
- no technical design;
- no implementation scope.

### Commercial terms

- R7,500 ex VAT;
- half a day on site or equivalent remote review where appropriate;
- credited against the next commissioned engagement under the existing terms.

### CTA

> Book a Process Review.

## 5. Offer sheet — Process and Automation Opportunity Audit

### Best for

A sponsor who needs evidence, prioritisation and a defensible investment recommendation.

### Promise

Identify and cost three operational opportunities, distinguish process and capability problems from automation opportunities, and recommend one next intervention.

### Deliverables

- sponsor-approved scope;
- one day of fieldwork across at least two operational levels;
- current-state map and evidence register;
- three findings ranked by value and delivery risk;
- visible calculations and confidence level for each benefit estimate;
- intervention classification for every finding;
- one recommended pilot, training intervention or process action;
- baseline, target and measurement plan;
- eight-to-fourteen-page decision document.

### Boundaries

- one process and one site for the standard audit;
- integrations are identified but not designed;
- no prototype or production changes;
- one recommendation, not a menu of possible projects.

### Commercial terms

- Standard: R24,500 ex VAT, two-week timebox;
- Extended: R48,000 ex VAT, three-to-four-week timebox;
- existing audit credit rules retained.

### CTA

> Quantify the opportunity.

## 6. Offer sheet — Fixed-Scope Operational Products

### Best for

A buyer with a familiar and bounded process that fits a published scope box.

### Approved product family

- Get Off Excel;
- Job Card System;
- SHEQ Incident Reporting;
- Contractor Compliance Register;
- Stock and Asset Register.

### Commercial rule

These products remain fixed-price only when every inclusion, exclusion, role limit and integration boundary is satisfied. If the client's version is materially larger, 2KO must route the opportunity to a Workflow Automation Pilot rather than discount or silently expand the product scope.

### Improvement requirement

Every implementation must record at least one baseline measure before go-live and one post-launch measure during the support period. A fixed-scope product is still an operational improvement intervention, not merely a software installation.

## 7. Offer sheet — Workflow Automation Pilot

### Best for

A bounded workflow with a measurable cost, a committed process owner and enough value to justify a production-grade proof.

### Promise

One workflow working in production against success criteria agreed before the build begins.

### Typical scope

- one process;
- one primary outcome metric;
- up to three user roles unless otherwise scoped;
- a defined data source;
- a defined human escalation path;
- a limited integration surface;
- four-to-six weeks.

### Deliverables

- confirmed baseline and target;
- improved future-state process;
- working production workflow;
- controls, audit trail and exception handling;
- user training and operating documentation;
- measurement dashboard;
- end-of-pilot result review;
- recommendation to extend, retain, revise or stop.

### Qualification rule

The current rule that a pilot should cost less than 25% of the annual value of the problem remains. A R145,000 pilot therefore normally requires at least R580,000 of credible annual value unless risk reduction or strategic value independently justifies the investment.

### Exclusions by default

- enterprise-wide rollout;
- unlimited integrations;
- replacement of a core ERP;
- consequential autonomous decisions without human approval;
- unverified generative AI use in safety, legal, HR or financial decisions;
- 24/7 managed operations.

### Commercial terms

- from R145,000 ex VAT;
- 40/40/20 milestone structure retained;
- fixed against the agreed pilot scope;
- rolls forward into the first build phase when the pilot succeeds.

### CTA

> Prove one workflow.

## 8. Offer sheet — Operational System Build

### Best for

A validated workflow that requires additional processes, roles, sites, integrations or control depth.

### Promise

Extend a proven operating model in sequenced phases without requiring the client to approve an unknowable enterprise total upfront.

### Commercial structure

- R350,000–R1.2m per phase;
- four-to-six weeks per phase where the existing timebox remains credible;
- each phase separately scoped and accepted;
- monthly milestone billing;
- no later phase is committed before the preceding phase establishes what is actually required.

### Mandatory phase acceptance criteria

- functional scope accepted;
- process owner acceptance;
- data migration or reconciliation accepted;
- control and audit requirements accepted;
- training complete;
- operating documentation complete;
- agreed outcome metrics visible;
- support and ownership transition agreed.

### CTA

> Turn the proven workflow into an operating system.

## 9. Offer sheet — Train, Improve and Embed

### Best for

An organisation that wants to build internal improvement capability while producing a measurable operational result and preventing the control plan from decaying.

### Joint delivery model

1. Six Sigma South Africa trains and coaches the cohort.
2. The cohort completes a live, sponsor-owned improvement project.
3. 2KO Systems assesses which controls should be automated or systemised.
4. A bounded system intervention embeds the approved control plan.
5. Sigmafy or a managed retainer tracks the project, control and ongoing result.

### Minimum conditions

- executive sponsor;
- suitable cohort and project pipeline;
- measurable baseline;
- authority to change the process;
- identified process and system owners;
- access to the required operational data.

### Commercial status

Custom and cross-divisional. Pricing must be built from the training cohort, coaching load, systems scope, licences and managed-improvement requirement. It must not be sold as a discounted bundle.

### CTA

> Build capability and embed the gain.

## 10. Offer sheet — Care

### Best for

A client that owns a 2KO-built system and needs dependable technical operation without an active improvement programme.

### Promise

Keep the live system healthy, monitored, backed up and supported.

### Included

- hosting administration;
- service monitoring;
- automated backup monitoring;
- security patching;
- next-business-day initial response during South African business hours;
- corrective maintenance for reproducible defects;
- monthly automated health summary.

### Excluded

- feature development;
- process analysis;
- training delivery;
- data correction caused by client input or third-party systems;
- third-party licence and usage costs;
- after-hours or 24/7 support;
- integrations not included in the commissioned system.

### Proposed terms

- retain R7,500/month provisionally;
- six-month minimum, then month-to-month;
- third-party costs billed under existing pass-through terms;
- annual escalation under existing terms.

### Commercial gate

The price remains viable only if average hosting, tooling, account administration and support effort remain below the internal cost ceiling established in the Phase 2 pricing model.

## 11. Offer sheet — Managed Improvement

### Best for

A client with one live system-enabled process that needs regular small improvements and active performance review.

### Promise

Keep the process measurable, identify the next constraint and deliver a controlled monthly improvement cycle.

### Included

- everything in Care;
- one named system and primary workflow;
- monthly performance and exception review;
- performance scorecard;
- prioritised improvement backlog;
- up to two planned delivery days per month;
- adoption and data-quality review;
- quarterly benefits-realisation review;
- up to two hours of targeted refresher enablement per quarter;
- unused delivery capacity may roll for one month only.

### Excluded

- new business process or unrelated system;
- major integration;
- formal accredited training;
- major data migration;
- dedicated on-site resource;
- 24/7 support;
- guaranteed financial outcome.

### Proposed terms

- approved public price: from R28,500/month ex VAT;
- six-month minimum, then month-to-month;
- quarterly scope and capacity review;
- additional work quoted at the published day rate.

### CTA

> Keep the process improving.

## 12. Offer sheet — Continuous Improvement Partner

### Best for

A sponsor who wants 2KO to jointly own the performance roadmap for one important operational workstream.

### Promise

Bring process, training and system expertise into one continuing operating rhythm, with a named lead and a visible benefits register.

### Included

- everything in Care;
- one bounded workstream;
- named improvement lead;
- fortnightly operating review;
- monthly management scorecard;
- root-cause and constraint analysis;
- improvement roadmap and prioritised backlog;
- up to five planned delivery days per month;
- coordination of process, enablement and system interventions;
- quarterly steering and benefits-realisation review;
- lightweight non-accredited team enablement connected to shipped changes;
- unused delivery capacity may roll for one month only.

### Excluded

- line-management responsibility;
- client staffing or performance management;
- formal accredited programmes;
- unrelated workstreams;
- large new systems or integrations;
- 24/7 managed operations unless separately contracted;
- guaranteed outcome where attribution is shared.

### Proposed terms

- approved public price: from R62,500/month ex VAT;
- six-month minimum, then month-to-month;
- quarterly scope and capacity review;
- additional project work separately scoped.

### CTA

> Put one workstream on a continuous improvement rhythm.

## 13. Offer sheet — Outcome Partnership

### Best for

A high-volume workstream with clean data, an auditable unit outcome and enough transaction value for both parties to share commercial risk.

### Permitted pricing structure

- a base fee that covers the minimum operating team and infrastructure;
- a fee per verified outcome, or an agreed share of incremental value;
- a cap, floor and reconciliation method;
- an initial proof period before outcome billing begins.

### Mandatory qualification conditions

- at least eight to twelve weeks of reliable baseline data, or sufficient transaction history to reconstruct it;
- metric controlled by a written definition;
- auditable event source;
- minimum transaction volume;
- client obligations and exclusions agreed;
- rules for seasonality, policy changes and external disruption;
- agreed human escalation and decision boundaries;
- finance approval of the benefit calculation.

### Automatic disqualifiers

- the outcome depends primarily on client staffing or discretionary decisions;
- no stable baseline exists;
- the client can alter the denominator or attribution rules unilaterally;
- the work involves autonomous safety, employment, medical, legal or credit decisions;
- the potential fee does not cover the operating cost and risk;
- success cannot be independently audited.

### Commercial status

Invitation only. No generic public price.

## 14. Qualification decision tree

Use the following sequence during every initial conversation.

### Question 1 — Can the buyer name the process and failure?

- **No:** Half-Day Process Review.
- **Yes:** continue.

### Question 2 — Is the cause understood and evidenced?

- **No:** Process and Automation Opportunity Audit.
- **Yes:** continue.

### Question 3 — Is the primary constraint capability or process discipline?

- **Yes:** route to Six Sigma South Africa or a Train, Improve and Embed design conversation.
- **No or mixed:** continue.

### Question 4 — Does the requirement fit a published product scope?

- **Yes:** fixed-scope product.
- **No:** continue.

### Question 5 — Can one workflow be isolated and measured in four to six weeks?

- **Yes:** Workflow Automation Pilot.
- **No:** audit, re-scope or decline. Do not jump directly to a large build with no proof boundary.

### Question 6 — Has the pilot proven the process and control?

- **Yes:** Operational System Build or rollout phase.
- **No:** revise, stop or select a different intervention.

### Question 7 — Does the client need reliability, improvement or workstream partnership?

- reliability only: Care;
- one-system improvement: Managed Improvement;
- one-workstream roadmap: Continuous Improvement Partner;
- independently auditable shared outcome: consider Outcome Partnership.

## 15. Qualification scorecard

Score each category from 0 to 2.

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Named process | Vague business problem | Broad function named | Bounded process named |
| Sponsor | No owner | Interested manager | Accountable sponsor with authority |
| Evidence | Anecdote only | Some artifacts | Reliable volume, time or cost data |
| Frequency | Rare event | Monthly/weekly | Daily or high-volume |
| Material value | Not quantified | Plausible but unverified | Clears the investment threshold |
| Process stability | Entire process disputed | Some variation | Current path understood |
| Data access | Not available | Possible with delay | Available and permissioned |
| Decision boundary | Unclear/high consequence | Mixed | Explicit human and system roles |
| User access | No user participation | Sponsor only | Operators and owners available |
| Sustainability | No owner after launch | Technical owner only | Process and system owners named |

Interpretation:

- **0–7:** do not propose a build; use a review, training route or decline.
- **8–13:** audit or discovery required before a pilot.
- **14–17:** suitable pilot candidate, subject to value calculation.
- **18–20:** strong pilot candidate and possible future retainer.

The score supports judgement; it does not replace it.

## 16. General commercial exclusions

2KO should decline or reframe work when:

- the buyer requests technology without access to the people who perform the process;
- there is no accountable sponsor;
- the project is an open-ended feature list;
- the buyer will not agree a measurable acceptance criterion;
- the requested automation would remove required human accountability;
- the client expects 24/7 managed service at a standard support price;
- procurement requires unlimited liability or outcome guarantees outside 2KO's control;
- a mainstream off-the-shelf product clearly solves the problem more economically.

## 17. Sales language

### Lead with

- the named process;
- the cost of the current state;
- the intervention decision;
- the baseline and target;
- the control that makes the change hold;
- the next smallest defensible commitment.

### Do not lead with

- developer days;
- an AI model;
- a generic platform;
- a long feature inventory;
- transformation language without a named process;
- discounts for unclear scope.

### Standard closing question

> Which process is costing you enough that doing nothing for another twelve months would be the expensive option?
