# 2KO Systems — Phase 3 Sitemap and Navigation

Status: Information architecture brief; no routes changed  
Date: 12 September 2026

## 1. Navigation objective

The current navigation gives Websites and Systems equal prominence and organises the business primarily by deliverable. The new navigation should organise the buyer journey around diagnosing, improving, automating and sustaining an operational process.

The primary navigation must answer:

1. What can 2KO help improve?
2. How does 2KO decide what intervention is appropriate?
3. What systems and automation can 2KO build?
4. What happens after launch?
5. Can 2KO prove the outcome?
6. What does engagement cost?

## 2. Recommended desktop navigation

### Services

- Process Review
- Process and Automation Audit
- Systems and Automation
- Managed Improvement
- Train, Improve and Embed

### Method

Direct link to the improvement lifecycle.

### Results

Direct link to verified case studies and evidence.

### Sectors

Direct link to sector-specific process problems.

### Pricing

Direct link to project and retainer commercial structures.

### About

Direct link to the current Studio content, reframed around process-improvement heritage.

### Primary CTA

> Bring us the process

The primary navigation should contain no more than these six text destinations plus the CTA.

## 3. Secondary navigation and footer

Place the following outside the main operational journey:

- Websites;
- Get Off Excel;
- individual productised systems;
- Scope Builder;
- Privacy;
- Terms;
- group and division links;
- contact details.

Productised systems may remain inside the Services menu under Systems and Automation, but their prices should not dominate the first-level navigation.

Websites should appear under a clearly separated heading such as **Other 2KO services** or **Websites for growing businesses**.

## 5. Redirect policy for later implementation

No redirect is required merely because a label changes.

If routes are renamed later:

- redirect `/studio` to `/about` only after the new About page is live;
- preserve `/quote` if it has advertising or external links, even if the label changes;
- do not redirect `/systems` to `/automation`; Systems and Automation is the broader parent;
- keep every product URL stable;
- preserve website URLs and remove them only from primary navigation;
- update canonical tags, sitemap entries and structured data together.

## 6. Homepage routing logic

The homepage should send visitors into four intentional paths.

### “I know what is broken”

Route to the appropriate fixed-scope product, pilot or Fit Call.

### “Something is wrong, but we do not know why”

Route to Process Review or Process and Automation Audit.

### “We already improved it, but the gain is slipping”

Route to Train, Improve and Embed or Managed Improvement.

### “The system is live and needs to keep improving”

Route to Managed Improvement or Continuous Improvement Partner.

## 8. Search architecture

The new structure should support four distinct search intentions.

### Process-problem intent

Examples:

- approval process automation;
- reduce manual reporting;
- operational process improvement South Africa;
- workflow bottleneck audit;
- continuous improvement consulting.

### Solution intent

Examples:

- workflow automation South Africa;
- operational software development;
- AI process automation;
- business systems integration;
- replace Excel with a system.

### Product intent

Examples:

- job-card system;
- contractor compliance system;
- SHEQ incident reporting;
- stock and asset register.

### Ongoing-service intent

Examples:

- managed automation service;
- continuous improvement retainer;
- software optimisation retainer;
- managed operational systems.

Keyword selection and search-volume claims must be validated later rather than inferred from these working themes.

## 9. Navigation decision summary

- Services becomes the main commercial grouping.
- Systems is relabelled Systems and Automation.
- Results becomes a primary destination.
- Managed Improvement becomes a first-class offer.
- Get a quote becomes Find your starting point in supporting contexts.
- Start a project becomes Bring us the process.
- Websites leaves the primary navigation but remains accessible and indexable.
- Group training and Sigmafy links become purposeful cross-routes rather than incidental footer references.
