## 1. Commercial purpose

The Integrated Improvement Partnerships are annual operating relationships, not discounted banks of development hours. They combine:

- Six Sigma South Africa training and certification value;
- active process-improvement consulting;
- bounded systems and automation capacity;
- Sigmafy programme and statistical-tool access;
- governance, scorecards and evidence;
- continuing responsibility for an agreed improvement portfolio.

System Care is a separate post-launch service and is not a substitute for an Integrated Improvement Partnership.

## 5. Training-credit economics

Training credit is redeemed at the prevailing Six Sigma South Africa corporate list value. It may be used for agreed public seats, corporate workshops, accredited cohorts or project coaching.

Required controls:

- credit is attached to the annual partnership and is not redeemable for cash;
- redemption is recorded against list value, delivery format and participant count;
- travel, venue, catering and unusual materials are separate unless included in writing;
- external accreditation or examination costs are recorded explicitly;
- the client may add training beyond the credit at the prevailing rate;
- unused credit expires at year-end unless the renewal schedule permits a limited carry-forward;
- training dates remain subject to faculty capacity;
- early termination, where contractually permitted, accelerates payment for credit already consumed.

## 6. Sigmafy economics and boundaries

Every package must state the product entitlement in the signed schedule. At minimum:

- tenant and company structure;
- named-user cap;
- active-cohort and project cap;
- SPC modules;
- file and data allowance;
- AI evaluation allowance and overage method;
- onboarding and configuration;
- support channel and response target;
- export, retention and termination treatment;
- any private deployment, SSO or integration requirement.

## 7. Gross-margin gates

Use a minimum 55% gross-margin target for the base annual agreement before discretionary outcome components. Mobilisation must also cover its direct delivery cost at the agreed margin.

| Partnership | Annual revenue | Maximum total direct cost at 55% gross margin | Monthly direct-cost ceiling |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Improvement Programme | R1,500,000 | R675,000 | R56,250 |
| Operational Excellence Partner | R2,700,000 | R1,215,000 | R101,250 |
| Transformation Office | R4,740,000 | R2,133,000 | R177,750 |

Total direct cost includes:

- improvement and technical delivery labour;
- training faculty, assessment and accreditation;
- Sigmafy infrastructure, AI and product support;
- directly attributable specialist support;
- directly attributable third-party services not recharged to the client.

Sales, leadership, product development, leave, general administration and unallocated bench capacity are funded from gross margin and may not be omitted when deciding whether the price is sustainable.

If the validated inclusion cost breaches the ceiling, change the allowance or the price. Do not rely on clients failing to use what they bought.

## 9. Mobilisation economics

Mobilisation is not free onboarding. It produces:

- validated baseline and target measures;
- selected workstream boundaries;
- governance and decision rights;
- data and integration assessment;
- benefits-register structure;
- initial training plan;
- Sigmafy tenant and programme configuration;
- first 90-day roadmap;
- risk, dependency and capacity schedule.

The Improvement Programme mobilisation is R150,000. Operational Excellence Partner mobilisation is R250,000. Transformation Office mobilisation is quoted after the diagnostic because portfolio size and data conditions vary materially.

## 10. Billing and risk rules

- fees are invoiced monthly in advance;
- mobilisation is invoiced before work begins;
- training credit vests against the annual agreement rather than as a refundable balance;
- the agreement states the treatment of training already consumed if the client terminates early;
- annual CPI or agreed escalation applies at renewal;
- one month of planned delivery capacity may roll once;
- unused training credit does not convert to consulting or cash without a signed variation;
- third-party and travel costs are passed through under the agreed schedule;
- material system builds remain fixed-scope projects;
- outcome components are invitation-only and require an accepted baseline and attribution method.

## 11. Proposal qualification gates

Do not sell an Integrated Improvement Partnership unless:

1. an executive sponsor is named;
2. at least one workstream has a defensible baseline or a funded route to establish it;
3. the client appoints operational and training owners;
4. data required for the scorecard is available or can be created;
5. the training population and likely programme mix are known;
6. the Sigmafy tenant, role and usage assumptions are recorded;
7. 2KO has confirmed senior-lead and faculty capacity;
8. direct cost passes the margin gate;
9. procurement accepts the 12-month operating model;
10. major system work is separated from retained capacity.
