# 2KO Umbrella Site and Retainer Blueprint

Status: Recommended strategic direction; ready for owner approval  
Date: 13 September 2026  
Supersedes: the site-specific brand and navigation decisions in the earlier 2KO Systems briefs where they conflict with this document

## 1. Executive decision

The new site should become the main `2ko.co.za` umbrella site.

It should not become a passive group directory or a generic corporate website. It should remain a sharp commercial site with one promise:

> **2KO makes operational improvement hold.**

The supporting proposition is:

> We improve the process, train the people, build the system and measure the result.

The current Systems positioning remains valuable, but becomes one part of a more complete operating-improvement proposition:

1. **Improve** the process.
2. **Train** the people who run and improve it.
3. **Automate** the repeatable work and controls.
4. **Measure and sustain** the result.

The site must make these capabilities feel like one connected operating model rather than four unrelated businesses.

## 2. Brand architecture

| Brand | Public role | Primary destination | What the umbrella site should do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2KO | Master brand and operational-improvement partner | `2ko.co.za` | Diagnose the need, explain the complete model and convert enterprise enquiries |
| 2KO Systems | Systems, workflow automation and AI-enabled operational controls | `2ko.co.za/systems` and `2ko.co.za/automation` | Demonstrate the systems visually and sell reviews, audits, pilots, builds and managed improvement |
| Six Sigma South Africa | Specialist training, certification and practitioner-led improvement | `sixsigmasouthafrica.co.za` | Keep the complete course catalogue, accreditation, schedules, enrolment and training SEO |
| Sigmafy | Improvement, training and statistical SaaS platform | `tools.sigmafy.co` or the final confirmed product domain | Keep the application and product-depth experience; receive qualified visitors from the umbrella site |

### Brand rule

2KO owns the client outcome. The divisions own specialist delivery.

The umbrella site may explain every capability, but it must not duplicate the complete Six Sigma course catalogue or Sigmafy product documentation. The specialist sites should remain authoritative for detailed evaluation and transactions.

### Naming rule

Use **2KO** in the primary masthead, footer, metadata and homepage language. Use **2KO Systems**, **Six Sigma South Africa** and **Sigmafy** when identifying the specialist capability or destination.

## 3. Positioning system

### Master promise

> **Operational improvement that holds.**

### Homepage headline direction

> **Improve the process. Make the result permanent.**

### Supporting statement

> 2KO helps organisations remove operational friction, build internal capability, automate repeatable work and keep the result visible. One partner across process, people, systems and measurement.

### Operating principle

> **Train where judgement matters. Automate where the rules repeat. Measure both.**

### Four capability verbs

| Verb | Buyer meaning | 2KO capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Improve | Redesign the work and remove the constraint | Process Review, Opportunity Audit and improvement programmes |
| Train | Build the capability to solve and manage problems | Six Sigma South Africa programmes and applied cohorts |

| Measure | Prove the benefit and prevent regression | Sigmafy, scorecards, evidence and managed improvement |

Every major page should connect its local offer back to this model without repeating the same layout or visual device.

## 4. Primary audiences and routes into the site

The site serves three distinct buyer modes:

1. **Enterprise operations leaders** who have a process, control or visibility problem.
2. **Capability and learning buyers** who need accredited training for individuals, teams or a corporate programme.
3. **Quality and improvement teams** who need statistical tools and a platform for projects, cohorts and evidence.

The first screen and navigation should never force these audiences to understand the group structure before finding their route.

## 7. Umbrella homepage sequence

The homepage should preserve the approved cinematic standard while changing the story in this order:

1. **Hero:** the master promise plus a large, believable operational interface showing the four capabilities converging on one process.
2. **Client proof:** the restored organisation strip, labelled as relationships across the 2KO Group.
3. **The operational problem:** waiting, rework, variation, missing evidence and decaying improvements.
4. **The four-capability system:** Improve, Train, Automate and Measure, each with its own visual language.
5. **A connected client journey:** diagnose → train/improve → systemise → measure → sustain.
6. **Division showcases:** one visually distinct feature for Six Sigma South Africa, 2KO Systems and Sigmafy.
7. **Results and evidence:** separate training reach, system demonstrations and verified outcome records.
8. **Managed Improvement:** recurring packages and the modular capability model.
9. **Sector fit:** process patterns across industries rather than a narrow industry claim.
10. **Starting point:** Process Review, Opportunity Audit or Fit Call.

The client strip should remain on the homepage and About page. It may appear in a reduced, static form on Training or Managed Improvement only where the wording accurately describes the relationship. Do not place the full group strip on individual system-product pages in a way that could imply each organisation bought that product.

## 10. Integrated Improvement Partnership cards

The primary recurring offer is not a software retainer. It is an annual operational-excellence relationship combining Six Sigma training, active consulting, systems and automation capacity, Sigmafy access, programme governance and evidence.

Use three public cards. **Operational Excellence Partner** is the preferred enterprise offer.

### Improvement Programme — one workstream and one developing team

**From R125,000/month ex VAT**  
**R1.5m annual commitment plus R150,000 mobilisation**

- one active improvement workstream;
- monthly operating and improvement review;
- three consulting or automation capacity days per month;
- approximately R300,000 annual Six Sigma South Africa training credit;
- Sigmafy team workspace within agreed user, cohort, project and AI limits;
- project charters, DMAIC tracking and statistical tools;
- process scorecard and benefits register;
- quarterly sponsor report.

### Operational Excellence Partner — two workstreams and an improvement portfolio

**From R225,000/month ex VAT — preferred**  
**R2.7m annual commitment plus R250,000 mobilisation**

- two active improvement workstreams;
- named senior 2KO improvement lead;
- fortnightly operating reviews;
- six consulting or automation capacity days per month;
- approximately R600,000 annual Six Sigma South Africa training credit;
- organisational Sigmafy workspace within agreed limits;
- full SPC and capability-analysis access;
- process, training and technology interventions;
- automation roadmap and bounded monthly releases;
- quarterly executive benefits review;
- priority access to relevant 2KO specialists.

### Transformation Office — enterprise improvement capability

**From R395,000/month ex VAT**  
**R4.74m annual commitment plus separately scoped mobilisation**

- three to five active workstreams;
- named 2KO programme lead and specialist delivery pod;
- ten or more consulting or automation capacity days per month;
- approximately R1.2m annual Six Sigma South Africa training allowance;
- multiple belt cohorts or a blended corporate curriculum;
- enterprise Sigmafy allowance within contracted limits;
- project, cohort, certification and benefits portfolio;
- statistical and process-governance support;
- systems and automation roadmap;
- monthly executive reporting and quarterly steering;
- optional performance-linked component where attribution is defensible.

### Separate system aftercare

System Care remains available from **R12,500/month ex VAT** for one named production system. It covers monitoring, backups, patching, service reporting and corrective maintenance under a defined service schedule. It is not one of the three strategic partnership cards and does not include training, improvement-roadmap ownership, Sigmafy or feature development.

After-hours or genuine 24/7 response requires a separately costed service schedule and should not begin below R25,000/month.

## 11. Partnership inclusions and commercial rules

### Training credit

Training is included as an annual credit at normal corporate list value rather than as an undefined course entitlement. The client may allocate it across approved public seats, corporate workshops, accredited cohorts or project coaching, subject to delivery availability and programme rules.

The credit:

- is not redeemable for cash;
- does not create unlimited training;
- vests against the annual contract and payment schedule;
- excludes separately identified travel, venue and third-party costs unless stated;
- expires at the end of the contract year unless a renewal schedule explicitly carries it forward;
- is supplemented through a separately quoted programme when the required cohort exceeds the allowance.

### Sigmafy allowance

Every partnership includes Sigmafy, but the schedule must state:

- named users;
- active cohorts;
- active projects;
- available SPC features;
- AI evaluation or usage allowance;
- support level;
- data, security and deployment requirements.

No package may promise unlimited users, AI usage, private deployment, SSO or on-premise delivery unless the product and commercial schedule explicitly support it.

### Consulting and automation capacity

Capacity may be used for process analysis, facilitation, statistical support, configuration, small workflow changes, automation and verification. It is measured in delivery-equivalent days including preparation and documentation.

- only planned delivery capacity may roll;
- rollover is limited to one month;
- major builds, integrations and migrations remain separately scoped projects;
- additional capacity is quoted at the current specialist delivery rate;
- client delay does not create an unlimited bank of work;
- incidents and support do not automatically create replacement improvement capacity.

### Mobilisation

Mobilisation is charged separately because the first stage includes baseline confirmation, workstream selection, governance design, initial Sigmafy configuration, data review, programme scheduling and the first 90-day roadmap. A recent paid diagnostic may be credited where its evidence is current and sufficient.

## 12. Qualification and capacity safeguards

- Every partnership names the process, workstream or portfolio boundary.
- Every engagement starts with a baseline, owner and decision cadence.
- Retainers do not include unlimited changes, support or training.
- Capacity is stated in delivery-equivalent days and rolls for one month only.
- A senior improvement lead should carry no more than 14 capacity units per month until delivery evidence supports a different limit.
- Formal accredited training is delivered through Six Sigma South Africa.
- Sigmafy inclusion must state users, workspaces, projects and support boundaries.
- Major integrations, migrations and new systems are projects even when discovered through the improvement backlog.
- Outcome pricing is invitation-only and requires measurable attribution.
- Integrated partnerships require a 12-month minimum term and are invoiced monthly in advance unless the signed schedule states otherwise.
- Training credits and platform allowances are entitlements within the annual agreement, not detachable cash balances.

## 13. Domain and migration strategy

Do not switch domains until the new umbrella routes, metadata and redirects are ready together.

Recommended sequence:

1. Build and approve the umbrella experience in the current repository and preview environment.
2. Inventory the indexed URLs and inbound links on `2ko.co.za`, `2kosystems.com`, Six Sigma South Africa and the Sigmafy product domain.
3. Confirm which existing `2ko.co.za` pages must be migrated, consolidated or preserved.
4. Make `2ko.co.za` the canonical master-brand domain.
5. Redirect matching `2kosystems.com` routes to their new `2ko.co.za` equivalents with permanent redirects.
6. Keep Six Sigma South Africa and Sigmafy on their specialist domains; use clear cross-domain analytics and referral tagging.
7. Update canonical tags, sitemap, structured data, Search Console and advertising destinations as one release.
8. Monitor rankings, crawl errors, lead attribution and branded search after launch.

No destructive overwrite of the current `2ko.co.za` site should occur without a content and redirect inventory.

## 14. Implementation sequence

### Release 1 — Umbrella foundation

- change the masthead and footer from 2KO Systems to 2KO;
- implement the new navigation and mobile information architecture;
- reframe the homepage around Improve, Train, Automate and Measure;
- preserve the approved dashboard-led visual standard and client strip;
- broaden About, Method and Results to the group level;
- leave specialist external sites intact.

### Release 2 — Capability pages

- build `/process-improvement`;
- build `/training` with the cohort-and-project hero;
- build `/sigmafy` with the full product-workspace hero;
- update Systems and Automation cross-links;
- ensure every page has a distinct product or operational visual rather than recycled cards.

### Release 3 — Commercial consolidation

- redesign Pricing around the single commercial ladder;
- implement the three Integrated Improvement Partnership cards;
- separate System Care from the strategic partnership comparison;
- publish training credits, Sigmafy allowances, mobilisation and capacity boundaries;
- update quote and contact routing for process, systems, training and Sigmafy enquiries.

### Release 4 — Migration and launch

- complete the domain content inventory;
- approve redirects and canonicals;
- validate analytics and cross-domain attribution;
- complete accessibility, responsive, performance and conversion QA;
- cut over `2ko.co.za` only after the launch gates pass.
