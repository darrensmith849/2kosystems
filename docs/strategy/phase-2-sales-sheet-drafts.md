# Half-Day Process Review

## Bring us the process that keeps going wrong.

Half a day with the people who do the work and the person who owns the result. We follow one process end to end, identify where it fails and tell you whether the right next step is process improvement, training, automation, a system—or nothing at all.

### You leave with

- a concise current-state view;
- the principal failure point;
- the likely operational impact;
- a clear build/do-not-build recommendation;
- the smallest defensible next step;
- a three-to-four-page decision memo.

### Scope

One process. One site. Half a day. No software pitch disguised as an audit.

### Price

**R7,500 ex VAT.** Credited against the next engagement you commission under the agreed terms.

### Start

Tell us which process keeps costing you time, money or control.

# Process and Automation Opportunity Audit

We examine the work as it actually happens, quantify three operational opportunities and distinguish problems that need process change or training from those that should be automated.

One process, one site, two-week timebox.

**R24,500 ex VAT.**

**R48,000 ex VAT.**

# Workflow Automation Pilot

## Prove one workflow before committing to a large system.

We improve and automate one bounded operational workflow, put it into production and measure it against success criteria agreed before the build begins.

### Suitable workflows

- approvals and escalation;
- job cards and field capture;
- document intake and validation;
- reporting and reconciliation;
- spreadsheet replacement;
- exception handling;
- classification, triage and assisted drafting.

### Included

- confirmed baseline and target;
- improved future-state workflow;
- production software;
- controls, permissions and audit trail;
- exception and human-escalation path;
- team training and operating documentation;
- result review at the end of the pilot.

### Time and price

**Four to six weeks, from R145,000 ex VAT.**

The pilot becomes the first phase of the full system if you proceed. If the result does not justify extension, you stop with working software and a measured answer.

### Start

Choose the process where one successful workflow would prove the case.

### Included

- a current performance view;
- one agreed improvement priority;
- a controlled change or intervention;
- a verified result or explicit learning;
- the next commitment.

# Train, Improve and Embed

## Build the capability. Deliver the gain. Embed the control.

A cross-group programme connecting accredited learning to a live operational improvement and the system controls that stop the result from decaying.

### How it works

1. Six Sigma South Africa develops the cohort.
2. Participants complete sponsor-owned improvement projects.
3. 2KO Systems identifies the controls that should be automated.
4. The approved workflow is embedded into daily operations.
5. Sigmafy or a managed improvement relationship tracks the result and control.

### Best when

The organisation wants improvement capability, measurable project results and a practical mechanism for sustaining the new standard.

### Commercial position

Custom. Training, coaching, systemisation and ongoing improvement are scoped transparently as connected components rather than hidden inside one discounted bundle.
