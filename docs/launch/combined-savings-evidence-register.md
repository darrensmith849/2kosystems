# 2KO combined-savings evidence register

This register is the internal substantiation record for the public statement:

> **$5bn+ in combined client savings influenced through process improvement.**

## Release status

- Public wording approved by the founder on 14 September 2026.
- The figure is a cumulative portfolio claim, not a single-client or single-engagement claim.
- The underlying project-level register must be consolidated and reviewed by the assigned evidence owner.
- Client-level values and identities remain confidential unless separate permission is recorded.

## Wording controls

Approved:

- “$5bn+ in combined client savings influenced through process improvement.”
- “2KO has helped client teams influence more than $5bn in combined savings.”

Do not use without additional evidence:

- “2KO saved clients $5bn.”
- “$5bn in independently verified savings.”
- “$5bn in annual savings.”
- Any named client's saving without written permission.

## Counting rules

1. Record one bounded benefit per row; do not combine unrelated initiatives.
2. Classify every value as realised, client-validated, client-reported, forecast or opportunity identified.
3. Record the original currency, measurement period and conversion basis used in the group total.
4. Do not count the same benefit again when it appears in training, consulting, coaching and system records.
5. For recurring benefits, record the annualisation method and never add multiple years unless the public claim explicitly uses lifetime value.
6. Record 2KO's contribution as lead, joint or supporting. The public “influenced” wording covers shared contribution; it does not imply sole attribution.
7. Link the source evidence and name the person accountable for confirming it.
8. Keep client naming permission separate from permission to include a value in the confidential aggregate.
