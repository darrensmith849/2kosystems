# 2KO organisation-count evidence

This note controls the public statement:

> **8,000+ organisations served.**

## Release status

- Public wording approved by the founder on 14 September 2026.
- The figure applies across the 2KO Group, not only the Systems division or one training programme.
- “Organisations” includes companies, public bodies and institutions.
- A deduplicated historical organisation register still needs to be consolidated.

## Partial source reviewed

Source: `White  Yellow Belt Online Delegates (Latest)(AutoRecovered).xlsx`

The workbook is a partial training record and cannot establish the group-wide total by itself. A read-only review identified:

| Measure | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Valid email rows | 2,362 |
| Unique email addresses | 2,261 |
| Corporate-domain email rows | 687 |
| Unique identifiable corporate domains | 240 |
| Public-email rows | 1,675 |

Public-email users cannot be assigned reliably to an organisation from the email address alone. Corporate domains also require normalization because subsidiaries and regional domains may belong to one group.

## Consolidation rules

1. Combine historical learner records, CRM records, invoices, proposals and client registers across every 2KO division.
2. Give each organisation one stable internal identifier.
3. Normalize subsidiaries, historic names, mergers and regional domains to a documented parent or operating entity rule.
4. Record the relationship type and source for every included organisation.
5. Do not count an organisation again when it appears across multiple courses, divisions or years.
6. Record the cutoff date, reviewer and reconciliation total.
7. Revise the public statement if the deduplicated eligible count does not support the published threshold.

## Approved and prohibited wording

Approved:

- “8,000+ organisations served.”
- “Organisations served across the 2KO Group.”

Do not use without narrower supporting evidence:

- “8,000 Systems clients.”
- “8,000 active clients.”
- “8,000 companies trained.”
