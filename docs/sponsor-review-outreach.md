# Sponsor Review — outreach

The page is built (`/review/<token>`). This is the covering email that carries it.

**Send it from your own mailbox, not the app.** This is a relationship email to
someone who already knows you. Sending it through a transactional system makes
it look like a campaign, and campaigns get read as campaigns.

---

## The email

**Subject:** `Your team's improvement projects — a look back`

> Nomsa,
>
> We pulled together the DMAIC projects your team submitted through the Green
> Belt cohorts. Six of them, costed at R1,646,000 — those are your people's
> numbers, not ours.
>
> One thing stood out when we looked at the control plans. Four of the six
> depend on somebody remembering to do something: a weekly check, a monthly
> reconciliation, a book signed at shift change. Two went into a system and
> will still be true in three years.
>
> I've put it on one page, private to Marula:
> https://www.2kosystems.com/review/<token>
>
> Nothing to action. If any of it looks worth a conversation, reply and we'll
> walk one of them end to end and tell you whether the gain held.
>
> Darren

---

## Why it is shaped like this

**Their numbers, said first.** The second sentence is a figure their own team
produced. That is the whole reason this is not cold outreach.

**One observation, not a pitch.** The manual-control point is the only claim
made. No services, no capability list, no "we'd love to help".

**One link, no attachment.** Attachments get stripped, quarantined, and not
opened on a phone. The link previews with their company name and their number.

**"Nothing to action."** Removes the obligation, which is what makes a busy
person actually click. The page does the selling.

**Short enough to read on a phone in the queue at Woolworths.** If it needs
scrolling, it gets postponed and then never read.

---

## What not to do

- Do not attach a PDF on the first email. Offer it if they ask.
- Do not mention price. The page carries it; the email should not.
- Do not send to more than one person at a company. Send to the sponsor. Let
  them forward it — the page is written to survive that.
- Do not follow up more than once.

## The single follow-up, ~8 working days later

> Nomsa — did this reach you? No reply needed if the timing is wrong.
>
> https://www.2kosystems.com/review/<token>

That is the whole sequence. If two of ten reply, the motion works.

---

## Before you send any of them

1. **Check the numbers against source.** Getting a client's own figures wrong
   is worse than never sending. Every rand value must trace to something their
   team submitted.
2. **Confirm the control-method reading.** "Weekly supervisor check" has to be
   what the control plan actually says, not an inference.
3. **Send to the sponsor whose company it is.** This works because it is their
   data going back to them. It stops working the moment it is anyone else's.
